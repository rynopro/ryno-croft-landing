      Frequently asked questions

      Straight answers.

      What is virtual staging?

      Virtual staging digitally adds furniture and decor to photographs of empty rooms so buyers can better imagine the space. Nothing is physically placed in the property.

      How much does StageReady cost?

      One image is $4.99. Five images are $19.95 ($3.99 per image) and ten images are $34.90 ($3.49 per image). All prices are in US dollars.

      Do I need a subscription?

      No. Every package is a one-time payment, with no subscription and no recurring charges.

      Can I choose the style?

      Yes. You choose the interior style and room purpose, and you can add specific instructions with your order.

      Can I use the images in my listings?

      Yes, subject to the requirements of your listing platform or MLS and any disclosure obligations that apply to you. You remain responsible for MLS, advertising and disclosure compliance.

      Are these physically furnished rooms?

      No. The images are digitally enhanced. Agents must disclose virtual staging wherever it is required.

      Can I request a demonstration?

      Yes. Qualified prospects can request one complimentary demonstration image. It is reviewed for suitability, carries a visible watermark and is not a full-resolution deliverable.

      How do I submit my photos?

      Through the secure order form on this page. Photos upload to private storage and are not publicly accessible.

      How good are the results?

      We aim for realistic, commercially usable images with natural furniture placement, believable lighting and the room's permanent features left unchanged. AI is not perfect, so a person checks every image before it is delivered.
