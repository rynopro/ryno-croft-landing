The Problem

The Bitter Truth: Great Products Can Still Have Weak Product Pages

You’ve spent countless hours perfecting your recipes, refining your small-batch process, and capturing product photography good enough to make people hungry.

But when a hungry shopper actually lands on your website, what do they read?

If your product descriptions read like a grocery store receipt — just ingredients and bottle sizes — you’re leaving money on the table.

In the craft food and drink world, customers don’t just buy a product; they buy an experience. They want to taste the smoky undertones, feel the velvety texture, and imagine the perfect weekend food pairings right through the screen.

If your copy isn’t doing that heavy lifting, mobile shoppers can bounce before they ever reach checkout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why a copy audit instead of a website redesign?

A visually attractive website can still have weak product messaging. The audit focuses specifically on the words shoppers read while deciding whether your product is worth buying — a lower-cost, lower-risk first step before you consider a larger redesign.

What platforms do you work with?

The audit can be applied to common ecommerce platforms such as Shopify, WooCommerce, Squarespace Commerce, Wix and similar ecommerce websites, provided the product pages are publicly accessible. This is a copy audit, not platform-specific technical development work.

How long does the audit take?

The completed PDF audit is delivered within 3 business days of confirmed payment.

No. The audit is based on your publicly accessible product pages. I’ll never ask for passwords, admin access or any sensitive account credentials.
