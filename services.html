Pilot, retainer and founding pricing

How the pricing works.

  • Each package starts as a 30-day paid pilot. If it works for you, you can continue month to month on a retainer at the same scope. Nothing renews automatically, and retainer terms are agreed in writing.
  • Founding client pricing: the price shown is held for your first 3 months in exchange for a written or video testimonial after delivery. I only publish a testimonial or case study with your written approval of the final wording. The standard monthly rate applies to clients after the founding period.
  • A 10% discount on the monthly rate applies if you commit to a 3-month or 6-month retainer in advance.
  • Delivery times are counted in business days from the day I have both your payment (or deposit) and your completed intake form.
  • The intake covers your voice and tone, your top three specialties, and who you want to reach. Please do not send patient or client names, records or photos.
  • Add-ons such as Instagram and Facebook caption adaptation and carousel slide copy are quoted separately in your scope. Design, scheduling and publishing are not offered.

How to order

No sales call required.

  1. Send a short enquiryChoose a package on the contact form and tell me what you need.
  2. Receive a written scopeYou get the deliverables, the fixed price, the delivery date and the payment terms in writing. Nothing starts until you agree.
  3. Approve and payYou are invoiced in US dollars by PayPal: in full for Essential Brand, or a 50% deposit for the other packages. No payment is taken through this website.
  4. Complete the intake and share your materialI send the intake form, then collect your notes, recordings or briefing.
  5. Review and reviseYou receive editable files and can request one revision round within 7 days of delivery.

What happens to clinical or market-specific claims?

They are flagged for your confirmation. Anything that cannot be verified is changed or removed before delivery.

AI use

AI tools may assist with research organization, ideas or rough drafts. All finished work is reviewed and edited by a person.
