"Expertise-led" can sound like a slogan, so here is what it means in practice.

It means the starting material is yours. Your observations, your explanations, the questions your clients ask you, the way you describe your work to a friend. A writer's job is to find the useful idea in that material and give it a clear shape.

## What it is not

It is not a request for a generic article on a popular topic. A piece that could have been published by any practice or any agent in any city does little to show what makes you worth choosing.

## How the work is done

First, a short intake covers your audience, your goals and how you like to sound. Then you share notes, voice recordings, old emails or answers to a few questions. From there, I look for the angles that will be most useful to your readers, write the pieces, edit them for clarity and tone, and send them to you for review.

## Where AI tools help

AI tools can help with organizing research, suggesting angles and producing rough first drafts. They do not replace judgment. Every piece is reviewed and edited by a person, checked against what you told me, and changed until it reads like something you would say. Claims that cannot be supported are removed or flagged for you to confirm.

## What it will not do

Content does not guarantee enquiries, sales or growth. What it can do is make your expertise easier to understand and easier to remember, on a regular basis, without taking your working day.
