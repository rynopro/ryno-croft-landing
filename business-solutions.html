You don't need to know exactly what service you need before you contact Ryno Croft. Start with the problem. We'll look at what is happening, what may be causing it, and whether there is a practical way we can help.

Different Problems Need Different Levels of Help

Not every business needs a large project. Some problems can be clarified through a simple audit or piece of research. Others require implementation, ongoing support or a more comprehensive project.

Solutions Session  →  Audit  →  Project  →  Larger Project  →  Ongoing  →  Product

These are different ways Ryno Croft can help — not mandatory stages. There is no requirement to move from one level to the next. Some businesses may only need advice. Others may need a focused project or hands-on implementation. The right level of help depends on the problem.

Sometimes You Don't Need More Advice. You Need Help Getting It Done.

Sometimes the answer is simply understanding what needs to change. Sometimes you already know what needs to happen but don't have the time, resources or expertise to do it yourself.

Where Ryno Croft can genuinely add value, the work can move from advice into practical implementation — but not every consultation leads to paid work, and that's fine.

You're Not Committing to a Project

The 30-minute Business Solutions Session is a paid, focused conversation — not a project commitment. You pay the R150 session fee and receive practical input either way. You can take the recommendations and implement them yourself.

If a bigger project makes sense and Ryno Croft is able to help, I'll explain what I recommend and give you a clear proposal. If there isn't a good fit, there is no obligation to continue beyond the session.
